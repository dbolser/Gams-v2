What is being simulated?

Dimensionless incompressible, resistive MHD (density = permeability = 1) on a 24³ periodic grid. Semi-Lagrangian transport, explicit field stretching and diffusion, and approximate pressure / magnetic divergence projection.

The initial magnetic field is a Hopf field: its ideal field lines are circles linked once. Lines are retraced through the current field; moving dots indicate field direction, not fluid particles.

This coarse solver has numerical diffusion even at η = 0. Periodic boundaries and discretization perturb the infinite-domain Hopf field. Energy and topology are not exactly conserved. The reset view traces the analytic field; evolution traces the grid.

How does the fibration work?

Each colored point on S² represents a circle on S³, shown here by stereographic projection into 3D. Distinct fibers link once. A latitude circle on the base sphere lifts to a torus; changing θ moves the sampled latitudes. The white highlight identifies the same fiber in both views.

This exact geometry stays fixed while the MHD field evolves. The exceptional fiber through the projection pole would appear as a straight line; it is omitted here. Dots show phase around a fiber, not fluid motion.
